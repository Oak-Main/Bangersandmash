Morecambe Bay Potted Shrimps

Tiny brown shrimps sealed beneath spiced, clarified butter — the taste of Morecambe Bay, served with hot toast for the butter to melt into.

Prep
15 min
Cook
10 min
Total
25 min
Yield
Serves 4 (starter)
Difficulty
Easy
Era
Georgian

Ingredients

  • 400g cooked brown shrimps (peeled)
  • 150g unsalted butter
  • ¼ tsp ground mace; pinch of cayenne; ¼ tsp grated nutmeg
  • Juice of ½ lemon; salt and white pepper
  • Hot buttered toast, to serve

Method

  1. Melt the butter gently and let it settle; skim off the foam and pour off the clear butter, leaving the milky solids behind.
  2. Warm the shrimps in the clarified butter with the mace, cayenne, nutmeg, lemon and seasoning for 2 minutes — do not cook them.
  3. Pack into ramekins and seal with the remaining butter.
  4. Chill until set, then bring back to cool room temperature before serving with hot toast.
