Pie, Mash & Liquor

The Cockney sacrament: minced beef pies with mash and “liquor” — a vivid green parsley sauce that has nothing to do with alcohol and everything to do with the East End.

Prep
40 min
Cook
45 min
Total
85 min
Yield
Serves 4
Difficulty
Moderate
Era
Victorian

Ingredients

  • Pies: 500g minced beef, 1 onion, 400ml beef stock, shortcrust base and suet or puff lid
  • Mash: 1kg potatoes, butter, milk
  • Liquor: 25g butter, 25g flour, 500ml fish or light chicken stock
  • Large handful parsley, finely chopped; splash of malt vinegar
  • Chilli vinegar on the table, as is proper

Method

Oven200°C400°FFan 180°CGas 6
  1. Simmer the mince and onion in the beef stock for 20 minutes; thicken with a flour slurry and season. Cool.
  2. Fill pastry-lined pie tins, lid, crimp and bake at 200°C / 400°F for 25–30 minutes until golden.
  3. For the liquor: make a white roux, whisk in the hot stock and simmer 5 minutes. Off the heat, add a mountain of parsley and a splash of vinegar — it should be unmistakably green.
  4. Mash the potatoes softer than you think wise.
  5. Serve pie and mash side by side, liquor ladled liberally over both, chilli vinegar to taste.
